Clear boundaries

Privacy policy

Slipramp does not currently include product analytics. The public website is hosted by Cloudflare and produces limited website traffic data. This policy explains both boundaries and the private data we will not collect.

Effective October 30, 2026

1. Your self-hosted Slipramp installation

The Slipramp project maintainers do not operate a service that receives repository contents, credentials or secrets, catalog definitions, run inputs or outputs, logs, artifacts, or other private control-plane data from your installation. Slipramp does not currently include product analytics or a call-home mechanism.

We may add product analytics in the future to understand how Slipramp is used and improve it. Any product analytics will not include repository contents, credentials or secrets, catalog definitions, run inputs or outputs, logs, or artifacts. We will update this policy and the product documentation before that collection begins.

Your deployment can communicate with systems you configure—such as Git providers, identity providers, object storage, and Kubernetes clusters. You operate those connections, and their own privacy terms may apply.

2. Visiting slipramp.com

slipramp.com is a static website hosted by Cloudflare. When you visit, Cloudflare processes the HTTP request and network information needed to deliver and protect the site. That includes your IP address, the requested host and path, request headers your browser sends, timestamps, response status, and bytes transferred. Cloudflare can use the IP address to estimate the request’s country.

Cloudflare makes aggregate traffic information available to us. That includes request volume, bandwidth, response status, and visitor or country estimates. We may use it to operate, secure, and understand use of the public website. It comes only from visits to slipramp.com; it does not include data from your self-hosted Slipramp installation.

Cloudflare may also ask compatible browsers to send network-error reports when they have trouble reaching the site. Those reports describe the failure and let Cloudflare provide network, country, and data-center reachability information. Cloudflare documents this in its Network Error Logging overview.

Cloudflare may provide hosting-level analytics as described above. We have not added any other analytics service or an advertising network, and the site has no accounts or forms. Cloudflare handles website traffic under its services and privacy terms. Learn more in Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

3. Information you choose to share

If you open a GitHub issue, contribute code, or otherwise contact the project through a third-party service, we can see the information you choose to submit. That service’s privacy policy governs its handling of your account and activity.

4. Links to other sites

slipramp.com links to GitHub and may link to other external documentation or services. We do not control how those sites collect or use data.

5. Changes and questions

If the project or website changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated and the effective date will change. For questions, open an issue in the Slipramp GitHub repository.
